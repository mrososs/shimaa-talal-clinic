## Design Context

### Users

The mobile app is used by Arabic-speaking parents and guardians, often one-handed and while travelling to or waiting inside a pediatric clinic. Their main jobs are to book and manage a child's visit, understand approval and payment state, follow their private queue position, message the clinic, and review parent-safe growth information without being exposed to staff-only clinical data.

The Dashboard is the clinic staff control surface; Mobile is the family's private self-service surface. Both must describe the same operational state in clear, non-technical language, while the API remains authoritative.

### Brand Personality

Warm, reassuring, and capable. The product should feel child-friendly without becoming childish, reduce anxiety during a clinic visit, and make consequential states such as “your turn”, “grace period”, “payment pending”, and “urgent request” unmistakable.

### Aesthetic Direction

Follow the creator-provided `Clinic Mobile App - All Screens.html` prototype: Arabic RTL, warm cream background, coral primary actions, teal success/secondary actions, softly tinted supporting colors, rounded but clearly structured surfaces, and the `Baloo Bhaijaan 2` / `Tajawal` type pairing. Use a light theme for the first release.

The production UI is an Ionic Vue mobile app, not a scaled desktop page. Use native Ionic navigation primitives, safe-area insets, touch targets, keyboard-safe forms, and a persistent bottom tab bar only for top-level destinations. Auth, onboarding, booking, detail, and other focused task flows stay outside the tab shell.

Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, cold clinical blue, decorative glassmorphism, excessive card nesting, hidden critical actions, and animation that competes with medical or queue status.

### Design Principles

1. Make the family's next safe action obvious; never imply a state transition the API did not perform.
2. Preserve calm through warm color, plain Arabic copy, stable layouts during refresh, and purposeful state-specific feedback.
3. Design mobile-first for 320–430 px widths, one-handed use, RTL, safe areas, dynamic browser chrome, and at least 44 px interactive targets.
4. Use Ionic navigation and platform behavior as the foundation, while applying the clinic's distinctive visual tokens consistently.
5. Treat accessibility and privacy as product behavior: visible focus, reduced-motion support, sufficient contrast, and no other family's or staff-only information in Mobile.
